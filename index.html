About Arcus 42

We believe that wealth is the by-product of value creation.

Co-founded by multiple families, Arcus 42 is dedicated to connecting families, industries and talent across the world, advancing the long-term development of family value in resonance with the well-being of humanity.

In an age of transformation, opportunity and uncertainty coexist. Economic and geopolitical shifts, rising regulatory complexity, the erosion of privacy, and growing expectations for responsible stewardship all redefine what it means to preserve legacy. The true challenge lies not only in capital management, but in clarity of mission and sustainability of spirit.

At Arcus 42, we believe that wealth is the by-product of value creation. Our mandate extends far beyond portfolio management, encompassing long-term strategy, global asset allocation, enterprise governance, succession education, philanthropic impact, and lifestyle administration. Across the full arc of legacy, we remain devoted to creating enduring value, for families and for the world they help shape.

The custodian of family ethos is the origin and destination of every decision. Only members embodied with foresight, integrity and boldness can steer their family legacies through the evolutions of time.

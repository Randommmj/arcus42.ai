COO

Terry Wang

王雁南

Venture Partner

Terry is a serial entrepreneur and seasoned investor who has founded two unicorn companies and served as an Investment Partner at ZhenFund.

He graduated from Princeton University with a degree in Computer Science and previously worked as an algorithm engineer at Microsoft and Uber.

CIO Office

Danny Lap Lee

李立明

Mr. Li Liming is an experienced investor with over 25 years of global investment experience in venture capital, growth, and buyout investments.

Mr. Li is currently a Senior Advisor at Blue Pool Capital. Since 2015, he has been a Partner at the family office, responsible for managing its global private equity investments. Mr. Li also manages the VCA Capital Fund I, which helps individuals and family offices manage their private equity portfolios worldwide through fund investments and direct investments.

Previously, he served as a Managing Director at Bain Capital and as a Vice President at Investor AB, the largest industrial holding company in Northern Europe. Mr. Li began his career in investment banking at Lehman Brothers in New York. He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Economics from Columbia University and is a CFA charterholder.

Mr. Li is a Finance Fellow at the Aspen Institute in the United States and an Edmund Hillary Fellow in New Zealand. He serves on the Investment Committee of New Zealand’s Elevate Venture Fund, is a board member of the Hong Kong Venture Capital and Private Equity Association, and is the Chairman of the Seed Foundation, a youth education NGO sponsored by the Alibaba Entrepreneurs Fund. He also serves as a committee member of the Financial Development Council, an advisor to the Hong Kong Trade Development Council and the Our Hong Kong Foundation, and a founding patron of the Hong Kong Palace Museum.

Maggie Chen

陈雪潜

Maggie has over 20 years of financial experience across Wall Street and Hong Kong, with cumulative investments exceeding USD 10 billion. She previously served as Chief Investment Officer at Jiachen Asset Management (Hong Kong), managing a USD 2 billion multi-asset portfolio. Earlier, she was a Fund Manager at China Hua’an Investment Co. (SAFE), overseeing a sovereign wealth fund portfolio of over USD 10 billion. Her career began at Morgan Stanley New York in Structured Bonds, followed by AIG’s Strategic Investments Department leading M&A target screening. In 2021, she founded Front Court Capital to provide strategic development and global asset allocation solutions for Asian family enterprises.

Maggie holds an MBA with highest honors from Yale University, where she studied under legendary investor David Swensen. She currently serves on Yale’s Greater China Advisory Board and as a Visiting Professor at the University of Hong Kong, fostering next-generation family enterprise leaders and bridging academic research with business practice.

Dafeng Guo

郭达峰

Dafeng Guo is a technology-driven investor with a strong Silicon Valley entrepreneurial background. He graduated from the Chinese University of Hong Kong with a degree in Computer Science.

In his early career, he co-founded Strikingly, a website-building platform that became the first Chinese team ever accepted into Y Combinator.

Dafeng has long focused on investments in digital assets, fintech, and artificial intelligence. With his engineering mindset, he excels at identifying global opportunities across both primary and secondary markets. He currently serves as a Director at the Vaulta Foundation, where he leads blockchain strategy and investment initiatives.

Advisory Team

Shahid Ahmed Khan

An influential figure in diplomacy, governance, and community leadership, currently serving as a Commissioner and Director of the Massachusetts Asian American Commission, as well as an Honorary Consul appointed by the President of Pakistan. He was awarded the “Sitara-e-Pakistan” (Star of Pakistan) in recognition of his outstanding contributions to public service and diplomatic affairs.

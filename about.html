About Arcus 42

We believe that wealth is the by-product of value creation.

Co-founded by multiple families, Arcus 42 is dedicated to connecting families, industries and talent across the world, advancing the long-term development of family value in resonance with the well-being of humanity.

In an age of transformation, opportunity and uncertainty coexist. Economic and geopolitical shifts, rising regulatory complexity, the erosion of privacy, and growing expectations for responsible stewardship all redefine what it means to preserve legacy. The true challenge lies not only in capital management, but in clarity of mission and sustainability of spirit.

At Arcus 42, we believe that wealth is the by-product of value creation. Our mandate extends far beyond portfolio management, encompassing long-term strategy, global asset allocation, enterprise governance, succession education, philanthropic impact, and lifestyle administration. Across the full arc of legacy, we remain devoted to creating enduring value, for families and for the world they help shape.

Governance innovation

Family Interest Oversight Committee

A key governance innovation of Arcus42. Comprising both family shareholder representatives and client delegates, the Committee serves as a collaborative and supervisory body responsible for ongoing alignment and co-creation. Its mandate is to ensure that Arcus 42 consistently upholds its values and delivers on its commitments.

It conducts regular evaluations of team, product, and risk management, offering recommendations and initiating special investigations when necessary.

It ensures succession of family inheritance goals, oversees culture implementation, and fosters an ecosystem with deep alignment and adaptive capabilities.

Composition

  • Family Shareholder Representatives
  • Family Client Representatives

Core Responsibilities

  • Regular review of core areas including strategy, products, and risk control

Unique Value

  • Long-term Interest Alignment: Family participation in strategy formulation
  • Principle Enforcement: Continuous oversight to prevent short-termism
  • Mission Commitment: Establishing long-term win-win mechanisms

Governance innovation

CIO Office

The second key governance innovation of Arcus42. Composed of investment principals from leading single-family offices, the CIO Office provides independent, buy-side perspectives in a marketplace often dominated by sell-side noise. Its purpose is to offer families objective insight and strategic allocation frameworks that reflect their true priorities.

CIO Office members bring first-hand family office experience, enabling them to understand the differences between family asset allocation and ordinary financial investments. They possess cross-cycle perspectives, transcend sell-side interest limitations, and provide asset allocation strategies and recommendations from the most independent and impartial viewpoints.

Unique Value

Incentives

Interest Alignment

Arcus42’s incentive mechanism centers on “long-term win-win cooperation,” adopting a fixed management fee plus carry model where team compensation is directly linked to clients’ actual returns.

Shared Carry Ledger

Sales and investment teams share results. Clients must first exceed the hurdle rate before Arcus42 receives performance compensation.

Win-win Rather Than Zero-sum

Institutional design mitigates short-term impulses and strengthens long-term compound growth thinking.

Behavioral Principles Embedded in Mechanisms

Incentive structures solidify the triangular roles of Architect, Reflective Ally and Connector, ensuring alignment of position, judgment, and action.
